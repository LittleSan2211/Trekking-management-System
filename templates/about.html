Our Story

About TrekPlan

We're building the most seamless trekking management experience in India. From booking your first trek to managing a full expedition roster — TrekPlan handles it all.

🎯

Our Mission

To make trekking accessible, organized, and memorable for everyone. We connect trekkers with expert guides and provide tools to manage every aspect of the journey.

🔭

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted platform for outdoor adventure management — empowering trekkers, staff, and administrators with smart tools and seamless workflows.

👥

For Trekkers

Browse available treks, filter by difficulty and location, book your slots instantly, and track your booking history — all from one beautiful dashboard.

🧭

For Staff & Guides

Manage your assigned treks, update slots and statuses in real-time, view participant rosters, and keep your profile up to date — everything at your fingertips.

⚙️

For Administrators

Full control over trek creation, staff approval, user management, and booking history. Assign guides to treks and monitor the entire platform from one dashboard.

🛡️

Built with Care

Developed using Flask, SQLAlchemy, and Bootstrap. Clean architecture with role-based access control, profile management, and a premium dark UI experience.
